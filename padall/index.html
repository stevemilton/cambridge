About PadAll

A new home for padel

PadAll Club is building a new home for padel — a place to play, compete and connect. We started in Haverhill because our founders were tired of driving across Suffolk to find a court, and we believe world-class padel shouldn't be a luxury.

Our mission is simple: make padel accessible and affordable for everyone in Haverhill, Cambridge and beyond. We keep prices fair, open our courts to local schools, and welcome complete beginners with free racket hire and intro sessions.

  • 🏟️

    Pro facilities

    Covered courts with professional surfaces and lighting for the best padel experience, whatever the weather.

  • 🤝

    Real community

    Open sessions, club socials and a welcoming crowd. Turn up alone and leave with a regular four.

  • 💷

    Fair prices

    Padel that doesn't price out the local community — affordable court hire, coaching and membership.

FAQ

Good to know

Where is PadAll Club?

Our first venue is at The New Croft Sports Hub on Chalkstone Way, Haverhill, Suffolk — with three covered padel courts and easy on-site parking. A Cambridge venue is coming soon.

How do I book a padel court?

Courts are booked through the Playtomic app or website — search for PADALL Haverhill, pick a time and you're set. You can also email haverhill@padall.com.

I've never played padel before. Can I still come?

Absolutely. Padel is the easiest racket sport to pick up, and our certified coaches run beginner intro sessions. Racket hire is available, so just bring trainers.

Can I play in winter or when it rains?

Yes — all three courts are covered with professional surfaces and lighting, so you can play in comfort all year round, whatever the weather.

What does membership include?

Priority court access, discounted rates, tournament entries and exclusive social events — plus the warm glow of helping grow grassroots padel.
