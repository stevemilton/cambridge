Our Story

"Why should we drive 45 minutes to play padel?"

That question started everything. Callum founded the club after realising Haverhill lacked local, affordable padel courts. After scouting locations through 2024, the club secured New Croft Sports Hub in 2025 — and is now building padel facilities for the whole region.

Our mission: make padel accessible to everyone in Haverhill and the surrounding areas. No cliques. No intimidation. No "members only" vibes — just quality courts and a welcoming community.

FAQs

Good to know

Where is PadAll Club?

We're at New Croft Sports Hub, Chalkstone Way, Haverhill CB9 0BW — with easy on-site parking.

How do I book a padel court in Haverhill?

Courts are booked online through Playtomic. Pick your court, choose a time and you're set — it takes under a minute.

I've never played padel before. Can I still come?

Absolutely. Padel is fast, social and incredibly easy to pick up. We run beginner introductions and group coaching, and rackets and balls are available to rent on site.

Do you offer coaching?

Yes — group coaching for different ability levels, personalised 1-to-1 sessions, and junior coaching in age and ability-based groups.

Is there a membership?

Our 100 Club membership is £297 per year and includes 18-day priority booking, 20% off courts and the club shop, and a free 30-minute coaching session.
