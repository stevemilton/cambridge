Weekly Shopping Lists

One pantry shop before you begin, then four weekly lists — used twice each across the eight weeks. Quantities cover both of you: his portions and her larger ones.

Before week 1: the pantry shop

Buy these once. They power all thirty-one recipes and most will last the full eight weeks. Roughly £35–45, amortised across two months of cooking.

Budget notes

  • Frozen fish and prawns are your friend. Frozen cod, salmon and raw prawns are typically 30–40% cheaper than the fish counter and nutritionally identical. Defrost overnight in the fridge.
  • Buy chicken breast in bulk packs (1kg family packs) and portion into freezer bags on shopping day.
  • Supermarket own-brand Greek yogurt, oats, tinned tomatoes and beans are indistinguishable from premium brands in these recipes.
  • Herbs: buy growing pots of basil, mint and parsley — they outlast cut packets by weeks. Freeze surplus soft herbs chopped into ice-cube trays with olive oil.
  • Expect the weekly shop (both of you, all meals and snacks) to land around £75–95 in a mid-range supermarket — usually less than the trolley it replaces.
