Weekly Planner

Pick a meal for each slot — or hit auto-fill and argue about it after. Every planned meal gets a one-click Add to Google Calendar link, and the whole week downloads as a calendar file (.ics) that imports into Google, Apple or Outlook in one go. The plan saves itself on this device.

Getting it into Google Calendar

Two ways, no accounts needed

One meal: tap “+ GCal” under any planned slot — it opens Google Calendar with the event pre-filled (meal, day, time, and a link back to the recipe); just hit save. The whole week: download the .ics file and import it at Google Calendar → Settings → Import & export — all planned meals land at once (breakfast 7:30, lunch 1:00, dinner 7:00, treats 8:30). The file also opens directly in Apple Calendar and Outlook.
