Desserts & Treats

Real treats, cleverly built — fruit, oats, yogurt, dark chocolate and nut butter doing the work of butter and sugar. Nothing here needs a teen portion note: everyone gets the same, and everyone’s happy. Macros are per serving.

Treat 01 · The freezer stash

Frozen Yogurt Bark with Berries, Honey & Dark Chocolate Drizzle

A slab of vanilla-honey yogurt studded with berries, snapped into shards like posh chocolate. Lives in the freezer for the “I want something sweet” moment — which is the entire point of it.

  • ⏱️ Prep: 10 min (+3 h freezing)
  • 🔥 Cook: none
  • 🍽️ Makes: 8 pieces (serving = 2 pieces)
140kcal
9gprotein
16gcarbs
4gfat

Ingredients

  • 500g Greek yogurt (full-fat sets creamiest)
  • 30g honey
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 150g mixed berries (raspberries snap best)
  • 30g dark chocolate (70%), melted
  • 10g chopped pistachios or almonds
  • Pinch flaky salt

Method

  1. Stir the yogurt, honey and vanilla together.
  2. Spread 1cm thick on a lined baking tray — thicker never freezes crisp, thinner shatters to dust.
  3. Press in the berries, drizzle the melted chocolate in stripes, scatter the nuts and salt.
  4. Freeze flat for 3+ hours, then snap into rough shards and bag them.
  5. Eat straight from the freezer — it softens to ice-cream texture in about a minute.

Thermomix

Melt the chocolate: 4 min / 50°C / speed 2 — glossy, never seized. Chop the nuts first: 3 sec / speed 6.

Keeps

Freezer, 1 month in a sealed bag. It never lasts a month.

For everyone

Let her design her own third of the tray — chocolate chips, banana coins, crushed biscuit. Ownership is the best sweetener.

Treat 02 · Lunchbox legend

Chocolate Banana Blender Muffins

No flour, no butter, no mixer — the entire batter happens in the blender in 60 seconds. Fudgy, properly chocolatey, and built from oats, banana and yogurt. Twelve muffins, gone by Wednesday.

  • ⏱️ Prep: 10 min
  • 🔥 Cook: 18 min
  • 🍽️ Makes: 12 muffins
145kcal
6gprotein
20gcarbs
5gfat

Ingredients

  • 150g porridge oats
  • 2 ripe bananas (~240g) — the spottier the sweeter
  • 2 large eggs
  • 150g Greek yogurt
  • 30g cocoa powder
  • 60g honey
  • 1 tsp baking powder + ½ tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • Pinch salt
  • 40g dark chocolate chips

Method

  1. Oven to 180°C fan; line a 12-hole muffin tin.
  2. Blitz the oats to flour, then blend in everything except the chocolate chips until completely smooth.
  3. Stir in most of the chips by hand (blended chips vanish; stirred chips stay molten pockets).
  4. Divide between the cases, top with the last chips, bake 16–18 minutes until a skewer comes out with moist crumbs, not wet batter.
  5. Cool 10 minutes before anyone touches them. This will be contested.

Thermomix

Oats 10 sec / speed 9; add everything but the chips, 30 sec / speed 6; chips in 10 sec / reverse / speed 2. Batter to bake in under two minutes.

Keeps

Airtight tin 3 days, fridge 5, freezer 2 months — 20 seconds in the microwave brings a frozen one back warm and fudgy.

For everyone

One in the lunchbox, one after training with a glass of milk. His portion note: one, with coffee, no guilt — it’s 145 kcal.

Treat 03 · Tastes illegal, isn’t

Date & Peanut “Snickers” Bites with Dark Chocolate & Flaky Salt

Sticky dates, roasted peanuts and peanut butter rolled into truffles and dipped in dark chocolate — the caramel-nougat-peanut experience rebuilt from four whole-food ingredients. Genuinely hard to tell from the chocolate bar.

  • ⏱️ Prep: 20 min (+30 min chilling)
  • 🔥 Cook: none
  • 🍽️ Makes: 16 bites
95kcal
3gprotein
11gcarbs
5gfat

Ingredients

  • 200g soft pitted dates (Medjool if the budget smiles)
  • 60g peanut butter (100% nuts)
  • 40g porridge oats
  • 30g roasted peanuts, roughly chopped
  • 60g dark chocolate (70%), melted
  • Flaky salt, to finish

Method

  1. If the dates are firm, soak in boiling water 10 minutes and drain well.
  2. Blitz the dates, peanut butter and oats to a thick, sticky dough. Pulse in the chopped peanuts last so they stay crunchy.
  3. Roll into 16 balls with damp hands; freeze 30 minutes to firm.
  4. Half-dip each in melted chocolate (half-dipped = maximum chocolate feel per gram), salt flake on top before it sets.
  5. Set in the fridge 15 minutes. Store where the teenager can’t see them, which doesn’t exist.

Thermomix

Dates + peanut butter + oats: 20 sec / speed 7, scrape, 10 sec / speed 7. Peanuts in 5 sec / reverse / speed 3. Chocolate: 4 min / 50°C / speed 2.

Keeps

Fridge 2 weeks, freezer 3 months — they eat well straight from frozen, chewier and arguably better.

For everyone

Two bites is a proper dessert. Great pre-tennis energy for her; his evening-snack swap when the day needs chocolate.

Treat 04 · 5 minutes flat

Peanut Butter & Banana Nice Cream

Frozen bananas blended into genuine soft-serve — creamy, scoopable, no cream involved. The peanut butter swirl makes it taste like a sundae; the ingredient list says otherwise.

  • ⏱️ Prep: 5 min (bananas frozen ahead)
  • 🔥 Cook: none
  • 🍽️ Serves: 3
180kcal
7gprotein
27gcarbs
6gfat

Ingredients

  • 4 ripe bananas (~400g peeled), sliced & frozen solid
  • 100g Greek yogurt
  • 30g peanut butter
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract · pinch of salt
  • 10g dark chocolate, grated, to serve

Method

  1. Blend the frozen banana with the yogurt, vanilla and salt, scraping down two or three times — it goes crumbly, then gravelly, then suddenly whips into soft-serve. Don’t add liquid; trust the transformation.
  2. Ripple the peanut butter through with two lazy stirs — a swirl, not a blend.
  3. Scoop into bowls, grate the chocolate over, eat immediately (soft-serve) or freeze 1 hour (scoopable).

Thermomix

Frozen banana + yogurt + vanilla: 30 sec / speed 7 with spatula assist, scrape, 20 sec / speed 5. The blades were made for this job.

Keeps

Best fresh. Frozen leftovers keep a week — 10 minutes on the counter before scooping. Keep a permanent bag of frozen banana slices; future desserts in 5 minutes.

For everyone

Her bowl takes sprinkles or crushed biscuit without shame. Post-tennis with extra yogurt blended in, it’s functionally a recovery shake wearing a dessert costume.

Treat 05 · Dinner-party grade

Dark Chocolate Yogurt Mousse Pots with Raspberries

Two ingredients do all the work: proper dark chocolate folded warm into thick Greek yogurt, setting into a mousse that tastes far more decadent than its label reads. Raspberries on top for the acid and the looks.

  • ⏱️ Prep: 10 min (+1 h chilling)
  • 🔥 Cook: none
  • 🍽️ Serves: 4
165kcal
9gprotein
14gcarbs
8gfat

Ingredients

  • 80g dark chocolate (70%), plus a square for shaving
  • 300g Greek yogurt, at room temperature (cold yogurt seizes the chocolate — this is the one rule)
  • 15g honey
  • ½ tsp vanilla extract · pinch of salt
  • 150g raspberries

Method

  1. Take the yogurt out of the fridge 30 minutes ahead.
  2. Melt the chocolate gently; cool 2 minutes until warm, not hot.
  3. Whisk the honey, vanilla and salt into the yogurt. Add the chocolate in a thin stream, whisking fast — it thickens dramatically as it lands. Stop at glossy.
  4. Divide between four little glasses or ramekins; fridge 1 hour to set to mousse.
  5. Top with raspberries and shaved chocolate. Serve like you went to more trouble than you did.

Thermomix

Chocolate 4 min / 50°C / speed 2; add room-temp yogurt, honey, vanilla, salt — 20 sec / speed 4. Straight into the pots.

Keeps

Fridge, 2 days, covered. Don’t freeze — the texture is the whole dish.

For everyone

Same pot for everyone; hers with a biscuit or wafer on the side. Nine grams of protein in a chocolate mousse is the plan working while pretending it isn’t.

Treat 06 · Sunday pudding

Warm Apple & Berry Crumble Pots with Oat-Almond Topping

Individual crumbles: soft cinnamon apples and burst berries under a golden oat-and-almond lid, with cold Greek yogurt doing custard’s job. The kitchen smells like October in the best way.

  • ⏱️ Prep: 15 min
  • 🔥 Cook: 25 min
  • 🍽️ Serves: 4
220kcal
6gprotein
30gcarbs
8gfat

Ingredients

  • 3 eating apples (~450g), peeled & chunked
  • 150g frozen or fresh berries
  • 15g honey + 1 tsp cinnamon + squeeze of lemon (filling)
  • 80g porridge oats
  • 25g flaked almonds
  • 20g butter, cold, cubed
  • 20g honey + pinch of salt (topping)
  • 200g Greek yogurt, to serve cold on hot

Method

  1. Oven to 190°C fan. Simmer the apples with the honey, cinnamon, lemon and a splash of water for 5 minutes until just softening; fold in the berries and divide between four ramekins.
  2. Rub the oats, almonds, butter, honey and salt together to rough clumps — clumps are crumble; crumbs are sand.
  3. Lid the fruit generously with topping. Bake 18–20 minutes until deep gold and bubbling at the edges.
  4. Rest 5 minutes (molten berry burns are a family tradition nobody needs), then serve with cold yogurt melting over.

Thermomix

Topping: oats, almonds, cold butter, honey, salt — 5 × 1 sec pulses / speed 5, stopping at clumps. Filling: apples 5 min / 100°C / reverse / speed soft with the honey and cinnamon.

Keeps

Assemble ahead (unbaked, fridge, 24h) and bake to order. Baked leftovers: fridge 2 days; 10 minutes at 180°C revives the lid.

For everyone

Everyone gets a pot; hers with proper custard when it’s been that kind of week. Cold-yogurt-on-hot-crumble converts sceptics at first spoon.
