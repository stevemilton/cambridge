Chapter 03

The 8-Week Plan

Four weekly menus, each run twice: weeks 1–4 then again as weeks 5–8. Every meal below links to its complete recipe.

How to read the plan

  • Tuesday and Thursday are training days (weights, 9–10am). Those days always carry a carbohydrate-forward breakfast and a rice, couscous or grain-bowl lunch. Add your post-training yogurt-and-banana snack at ~10:15am.
  • “×2” means cook a double batch — the second portions become a later lunch or go into the freezer.
  • ❄️ means tonight’s meal comes from the freezer, cooked in an earlier week. Move it to the fridge the night before.
  • Breakfasts follow the same weekly rhythm every week — quick on weekdays, cooked and social at the weekend.
  • Tennis slots wherever it lands in your week; just add carbs before and fluids and protein after (see Chapter 02).
  • One meal per week, swap anything for a family meal out or takeaway of your choosing. It’s in the design.

Weeks 5–8

Repeat weeks 1–4 exactly. By the second cycle you will know the recipes well enough to cook most of them without reading, the shopping lists will take ten minutes, and the freezer rotation will already be stocked from the habit you built in the first month. If any meal has become a chore, swap it for another from the same chapter — like for like (grain bowl for grain bowl, traybake for traybake) keeps the numbers honest.

Checkpoint at week 4

Average your morning weigh-ins for week 4 and compare with week 1. Down 1.5–2.5kg? Perfect — change nothing. Down more than 3kg? Add 100–150 kcal back (a bigger breakfast is the best place). Down less than 1kg? Check the measured items first — oil, rice, nuts — before cutting anything.
