How to use this book

The one-menu, two-portions rule

Every recipe is written with two portion columns in mind. The macros shown are for the adult fat-loss portion. Every recipe then carries a “For your daughter” note: same food, larger carbohydrate serving, and never a calorie count — she is growing and training, not dieting.

Before you start

This plan was designed for a healthy 86kg adult male aiming to lose roughly 0.5kg per week. It is general guidance, not medical advice. If you have any medical condition, or any concerns about a teenager’s eating, speak to your GP or a registered dietitian first.
