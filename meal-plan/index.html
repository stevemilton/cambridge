How the portions work

One meal, two plates

Macros shown are the adult portion (~2,000 kcal & 150–170g protein across the day, for steady fat loss with twice-weekly training). Every recipe ends with a “Teen plate” line: same food, bigger carbs — extra rice, an extra wrap, more chips — plus free snacks. She eats to appetite, never to numbers.

The daily rhythm (his)

Breakfast ~420 kcal · mid-morning snack: 250g Greek yogurt + honey + banana (post-gym on training days) · lunch ~530 kcal · dinner ~640 kcal · evening snack: cottage cheese or yogurt. That’s ~2,000 kcal and 150g+ protein without weighing anything at the table.
