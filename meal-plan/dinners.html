Dinner 01 · The signature

Garlic & Lemon Chicken with Crispy Herb Potatoes & Charred Broccoli

Proof that chicken, potatoes and broccoli can be the best thing you eat all week: fierce garlic-lemon marinade, glass-crisp rosemary potatoes, and a 90-second pan sauce made from the marinade itself.

  • ⏱️ Prep: 15 min (+30 min marinating)
  • 🔥 Cook: 40 min
  • 🍽️ Serves: 2
640kcal
52gprotein
55gcarbs
20gfat

Ingredients

  • 2 × 200g chicken breasts, butterflied
  • 15ml olive oil + 4 garlic cloves + 1 lemon (zest & juice, halves kept) + 2 tsp oregano + 1 tsp Dijon + 1 tsp honey (marinade)
  • 500g baby new potatoes, halved
  • 15ml olive oil + rosemary & thyme + 1 tsp flaky salt (potatoes)
  • 200g Tenderstem broccoli + 5ml oil + 1 garlic clove + chilli flakes

Method

  1. Whisk the marinade; pour two-thirds over the chicken (reserve the rest — it becomes the sauce). 30 min minimum, overnight better.
  2. 210°C fan. Parboil potatoes 8 minutes, drain and shake hard until the edges scuff — scuffed edges are where crisp comes from. Toss with oil, herbs and salt; roast cut-side down 30–35 minutes, untouched for the first 20.
  3. Broccoli onto the tray for the last 12 minutes, tossed with its oil, garlic and chilli.
  4. Smoking-hot griddle: chicken 4–5 minutes per side until charred and cooked through (74°C). Char the spent lemon halves alongside. Rest 4 minutes.
  5. Boil the reserved marinade with 3 tbsp water and the resting juices for 90 seconds until glossy. Slice the chicken, sauce over, lemon halves for squeezing over everything.

Thermomix

Marinade for the week in one hit: garlic 3 sec / speed 7, add oil, lemon, oregano, Dijon, honey 10 sec / speed 4. Make triple and freeze chicken flat in bags of it.

Teen plate

Roast an extra handful of potatoes — her plate takes 250–300g to your 200g — plus garlic bread on training days.

Keeps

Cooked chicken 3 days (great cold in wraps). Potatoes re-crisp in the oven, never the microwave.

Dinner 02 · 25 minutes · The TikTok one

Sticky Honey-Garlic Salmon Rice Bowl with Steamed Broccoli & Sesame

The viral salmon rice bowl, done properly: crispy-edged salmon chunks lacquered in a honey-soy-garlic glaze that turns to caramel in the pan, over rice with broccoli and sesame. Twenty-five minutes, start to bowl.

  • ⏱️ Prep: 10 min
  • 🔥 Cook: 15 min
  • 🍽️ Serves: 2
620kcal
42gprotein
60gcarbs
22gfat

Ingredients

  • 2 × 140g salmon fillets, skinned, 3cm chunks
  • 1 tbsp cornflour (the crisp trick)
  • 10ml olive oil
  • 150g basmati rice
  • 250g broccoli florets
  • 1 tsp sesame seeds · spring onion · cucumber, sliced

Glaze

  • 25ml soy sauce + 20g honey
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed + 1 tsp grated ginger
  • 1 tsp rice or red wine vinegar + 40ml water
  • Sriracha to taste (optional, correct)

Method

  1. Rice on. Steam the broccoli until just tender (bright green, slight bite).
  2. Pat the salmon chunks dry, dust with the cornflour — this is what makes the glaze cling and the edges crisp.
  3. Hot pan, oil: salmon chunks 90 seconds per side until golden all over. Gently — they’re delicate now.
  4. Glaze in: it bubbles, thickens and turns to caramel in about 90 seconds. Roll the salmon in it off the heat.
  5. Rice, broccoli, sticky salmon and every drop of glaze. Sesame, spring onion, cucumber on the side of the bowl.

Thermomix

Full side-dish autopilot: rice in the basket, broccoli in the Varoma above — 16 min / Varoma / speed 4. Both done the moment the salmon is.

Teen plate

200g rice for the pot instead of 150g, the bigger scoop hers, plus extra glaze — make 1.5× and nobody complains.

Keeps

Best eaten fresh; leftover salmon is good cold next day over rice. Rice: 24-hour rule, cool it fast.

Dinner 03 · Batch & freeze

Turkey Ragu Spaghetti with Hidden Veg & Parmesan

A slow-tasting ragu in under an hour, with finely grated vegetables that melt into the sauce and make it silkier and sweeter. Nobody has ever detected the courgette. Makes four portions — half goes to the freezer.

  • ⏱️ Prep: 15 min
  • 🔥 Cook: 50 min
  • 🍽️ Serves: 4 (eat 2, freeze 2)
630kcal
48gprotein
68gcarbs
14gfat

Ingredients (4 portions of sauce)

  • 500g turkey mince (2% fat)
  • 15ml olive oil
  • 1 onion + 2 carrots + 1 courgette + 150g mushrooms, all fine
  • 4 garlic cloves + 2 tbsp tomato purée
  • 2 × 400g tins chopped tomatoes + 200ml stock
  • 1 tsp oregano + 1 bay leaf + 1 tsp balsamic + ½ tsp honey
  • 220g wholemeal spaghetti for tonight (90g him / 130g her)
  • 20g parmesan · basil

Method

  1. Brown the turkey hard in the oil, in two batches, leaving it alone long enough to catch bronze. Set aside.
  2. Soften the chopped/grated onion, carrot, courgette and mushrooms with a pinch of salt, 8–10 minutes, until collapsed and nearly dry.
  3. Garlic 1 minute, purée 2 minutes until it darkens. Turkey back in with tomatoes, stock, oregano, bay, balsamic, honey.
  4. Simmer, lid ajar, 30–35 minutes until thick enough to hold a spoon-trail.
  5. Spaghetti to 1 minute under packet time; toss with half the ragu and a splash of pasta water until glossy. Parmesan, basil. Freeze the other half.

Thermomix

The veg base: onion, carrot, courgette, mushrooms, garlic 5 sec / speed 5, then sauté 8 min / 120°C / speed 1. Brown the mince in a pan for proper colour, add to the bowl with everything else: 30 min / 100°C / reverse / speed soft, cap off.

Teen plate

130g spaghetti to your 90g, garlic bread on request (there will be a request), cheese without accountancy.

Keeps

Ragu: fridge 3 days (better on day two), freezer 3 months in flat bags. Cook pasta fresh each time — 10 minutes, 90% of the eating quality.

Dinner 04 · Build-your-own night

Chicken Fajita Traybake with Charred Peppers, Warm Tortillas & Lime Crema

Everything on one tray — smoky spiced chicken strips and three colours of pepper, edges catching in the oven — then straight to the table with warm tortillas and bowls of toppings. Build-your-own means everybody wins.

  • ⏱️ Prep: 15 min
  • 🔥 Cook: 25 min
  • 🍽️ Serves: 2
620kcal
46gprotein
55gcarbs
18gfat

Ingredients

  • 380g chicken breast, thick strips
  • 3 peppers (mixed colours), strips + 1 red onion, wedges
  • 15ml olive oil
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika + 1 tsp cumin + ½ tsp garlic powder + ½ tsp oregano + pinch of cayenne (fajita mix — no sachets needed)
  • 1 lime — half juiced over the tray, half in wedges
  • 4 wholemeal tortillas, warmed

Table toppings

  • 100g Greek yogurt + lime zest (crema)
  • ½ avocado, sliced · fresh salsa (tomato, onion, coriander, lime)
  • 30g grated cheddar

Method

  1. 220°C fan. Toss the chicken, peppers and onion on the tray with the oil and every grain of the spice mix.
  2. Roast 20–25 minutes, one good toss halfway, until the chicken is cooked and the pepper edges are properly charred — pale fajitas are sad fajitas.
  3. Lime juice over the hot tray — it hisses and lifts everything.
  4. Warm the tortillas (30 seconds in a dry pan each, or foil-wrapped in the oven’s last 5 minutes).
  5. Tray to the table with the toppings in bowls. Him: 2 tortillas, heavy on chicken. Her: see below.

Thermomix

Salsa 3 sec / speed 4; crema 5 sec / speed 3; cheddar 3 sec / speed 7. Three toppings, one bowl, rinse between.

Teen plate

Three tortillas, the cheese, the avocado — the full works. Fajita night needs no persuasion.

Keeps

Leftover tray filling: 3 days — tomorrow’s lunch over rice as a burrito bowl. The spice mix scales ×10 into a jar.

Dinner 05 · Fakeaway · The katsu

Baked Chicken Katsu Curry with Sticky Rice & Cucumber Salad

Crunchy panko chicken — baked, not fried, and none the worse for it — under a silky, gently spiced katsu sauce blended from onion, carrot, ginger and curry powder. The Thermomix makes the sauce like it was born to.

  • ⏱️ Prep: 20 min
  • 🔥 Cook: 30 min
  • 🍽️ Serves: 2
650kcal
48gprotein
68gcarbs
16gfat

Ingredients

  • 2 × 180g chicken breasts, butterflied & lightly flattened
  • 50g panko breadcrumbs + 1 egg, beaten + 2 tbsp flour
  • 10ml olive oil (for the crumbs)
  • 150g basmati or sushi rice
  • ½ cucumber, thin half-moons + 1 tsp rice vinegar + pinch of sugar & salt

Katsu sauce

  • 1 onion + 2 carrots (~180g), chunks
  • 2 garlic cloves + 1 tsp grated ginger
  • 5ml oil + 1 tbsp mild curry powder + ½ tsp turmeric
  • 400ml chicken stock + 1 tsp honey + 1 tsp soy sauce

Method

  1. 210°C fan. Toast the panko with the oil in a dry pan for 2–3 minutes until golden — this is the secret to “fried” colour from the oven.
  2. Flour, egg, golden panko: coat the chicken, press the crumbs on, onto a rack over a tray. Bake 20–22 minutes until cooked (74°C) and crackling.
  3. Sauce: soften the onion, carrot, garlic and ginger in the oil 8 minutes; curry powder and turmeric 1 minute; stock, honey, soy; simmer 12 minutes until the carrots give. Blend completely smooth.
  4. Rice on meanwhile; scrunch the cucumber with the vinegar, sugar and salt.
  5. Slice the katsu on the diagonal, lay over rice, flood one side with sauce. Cucumber salad on the rim.

Thermomix

The whole sauce, hands-free: onion, carrot, garlic, ginger 5 sec / speed 5; sauté 8 min / 120°C / speed 1; add spices 1 min / 120°C / speed 1; stock, honey, soy 12 min / 100°C / speed 1; then blend 1 min / speed 8. Restaurant-smooth, zero pans.

Teen plate

200g rice in the pot, her scoop bigger, and extra sauce — make 1.5× because everyone always wants more katsu sauce.

Keeps

Sauce: fridge 4 days, freezer 3 months — batch it. Crumbed chicken: crumb ahead (raw, fridge, 24h), bake fresh.

Dinner 06 · Friday night

Turkey Smash Burgers with Oven Chips & Burger Sauce

Burger night stays. Thin-smashed turkey patties with proper crust, melted cheddar, a paprika-yogurt burger sauce nobody clocks as yogurt, and real chips from real potatoes.

  • ⏱️ Prep: 20 min
  • 🔥 Cook: 35 min
  • 🍽️ Serves: 2
655kcal
47gprotein
62gcarbs
19gfat

Ingredients

  • 400g turkey mince (2% fat)
  • ½ onion, grated & squeezed + 1 garlic clove + 1 tsp oregano + ¾ tsp salt
  • 2 slices cheddar (~40g)
  • 2 wholemeal buns, toasted
  • 600g potatoes, 1.5cm chips + 15ml oil + 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • Little Gem, tomato, pickled onion (to build)

Burger sauce

  • 80g Greek yogurt + 1 tsp tomato purée + ½ tsp smoked paprika + ½ tsp honey + 1 tsp finely chopped gherkin

Method

  1. Chips first: soak cut chips 10 minutes, dry completely, toss with oil, paprika, salt. 210°C fan, single layer, 30–35 minutes, turned once.
  2. Mix the mince, onion, garlic, oregano and salt; divide into 4 loose balls. Stir the sauce.
  3. Screaming-hot pan: ball in, smash flat and thin with a spatula, 2 minutes until the edges frill and crust, flip, cheese on, 90 seconds. Two thin patties per bun beats one thick one.
  4. Toast the buns cut-side down in the burger pan.
  5. Build: sauce, lettuce, double patty, tomato, pickled onion, more sauce, lid. Chips alongside. No cutlery — house rules.

Thermomix

Not for smashing — but the sauce (5 sec / speed 3) and a quick pickled-onion chop (3 sec / speed 4 with a splash of vinegar) happen while the pan heats.

Teen plate

Same double-patty burger, the bigger pile of chips, and a milkshake blitzed from milk, frozen banana and honey — Thermomix: 1 min / speed 8.

Keeps

Raw patties: fridge 24h, freezer 3 months between paper squares. Cooked: 3 days — tomorrow’s salad topper.

Dinner 07 · Saturday night

Steak Night with Chimichurri, Charred Peppers & Garlic-Crushed Potatoes

A trimmed sirloin is lean; chimichurri makes 220g of it an occasion. Dry surface, hot pan, full rest — the holy trinity — and the sharp green sauce runs into everything else on the plate.

  • ⏱️ Prep: 15 min
  • 🔥 Cook: 25 min
  • 🍽️ Serves: 2
635kcal
50gprotein
48gcarbs
24gfat

Ingredients

  • 2 × 220g sirloin steaks, trimmed, 2.5cm thick
  • 450g baby potatoes + 2 garlic cloves + 10ml olive oil
  • 3 peppers, quartered · 100g green beans

Chimichurri

  • 25g flat-leaf parsley, chopped fine
  • 1 tbsp fresh oregano (or 1 tsp dried) + 2 garlic cloves + ½ red chilli
  • 2 tbsp red wine vinegar + 20ml extra virgin olive oil + ½ tsp flaky salt

Method

  1. Chimichurri first — it needs 20 minutes for the vinegar to marry the herbs. Loose and vivid, not blended smooth.
  2. Steaks out of the fridge 30 minutes ahead. Peppers roast at 210°C for 20–25 minutes.
  3. Boil potatoes 14–16 minutes (beans in for the last 3); drain, crush lightly, fold with garlic, oil, salt.
  4. Steaks patted bone-dry, oiled, seasoned heavily. Pan just short of smoking: 2½ minutes per side untouched for medium-rare. Tongs, never a fork.
  5. Rest 5 full minutes. Slice against the grain, chimichurri spooned over, resting juices into the sauce.

Thermomix

Chimichurri without knife-work: parsley, oregano, garlic, chilli — 3 × 1 sec pulses / speed 5 (stop while it still has texture), then vinegar, oil, salt 5 sec / reverse / speed 2.

Teen plate

Same steak — she needs the iron more than anyone at the table. 300g of the potatoes to your 200g, buttered corn in season.

Keeps

Chimichurri: fridge 3 days, deepens. Leftover steak (theoretical) slices cold into tomorrow’s wrap.

Dinner 08 · 25 minutes · The creamy one

Creamy Cajun Chicken Pasta with Peppers & Spinach

The creamy pasta the internet loves, quietly rebuilt: smoky cajun-spiced chicken, peppers, and a sauce of light crème fraîche and parmesan that eats far richer than it reads. One pan plus a pasta pot, 25 minutes.

  • ⏱️ Prep: 10 min
  • 🔥 Cook: 18 min
  • 🍽️ Serves: 2
640kcal
48gprotein
62gcarbs
18gfat

Ingredients

  • 350g chicken breast, strips
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika + 1 tsp garlic powder + 1 tsp oregano + ½ tsp thyme + cayenne to taste (cajun mix — make it yourself, skip the sachet salt-bomb)
  • 10ml olive oil
  • 180g wholemeal penne (80g him / 100g her)
  • 1 red pepper, strips + 1 small onion, sliced + 2 garlic cloves
  • 120ml half-fat crème fraîche + 60ml pasta water
  • 20g parmesan, grated + 80g baby spinach

Method

  1. Toss the chicken in the oil and two-thirds of the cajun mix. Pasta on; steal a mug of its water before draining.
  2. Hot pan: chicken 5–6 minutes until burnished and cooked. Out.
  3. Same pan: pepper and onion 4 minutes, garlic and remaining spice mix 1 minute.
  4. Heat down: crème fraîche, a splash of pasta water and the parmesan, stirred to a sauce. Spinach in — it collapses on contact.
  5. Pasta and chicken in; toss until everything is coated, loosening with pasta water to a silky cling. Black pepper, more parmesan, bowls.

Thermomix

Grate the parmesan (10 sec / speed 10) and blitz a big jar of the cajun mix ×10 (10 sec / speed 8) — future-you shakes it straight on.

Teen plate

Her 100g of pasta is built in; add garlic bread and extra parmesan. This is routinely voted the best thing on this page.

Keeps

Fridge 2 days; reheat gently with a splash of milk so the sauce relaxes instead of splitting.
