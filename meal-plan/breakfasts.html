Breakfast 01 · 10 minutes

Berry & Banana Protein Smoothie Bowl with Crunchy Toppings

Thick enough to hold its toppings — that’s the difference between a smoothie bowl and a smoothie in a bowl. Frozen fruit and minimal liquid is the trick.

  • ⏱️ Prep: 10 min
  • 🔥 Cook: none
  • 🍽️ Serves: 2
420kcal
30gprotein
55gcarbs
9gfat

Ingredients

  • 250g frozen mixed berries
  • 1 frozen banana (~100g), chunks
  • 400g 0% fat Greek yogurt
  • 60ml milk (just enough to blend)
  • 40g porridge oats
  • 15g honey

Toppings

  • 40g granola or oat crunch
  • 80g fresh berries
  • 10g mixed seeds

Method

  1. Blend the frozen berries, banana, yogurt, milk, oats and honey until completely smooth, scraping down once. It should be soft-serve thick — resist adding more milk.
  2. Spoon into two wide bowls (spoon, not pour — if it pours, it’s too thin).
  3. Top in neat rows: granola, fresh berries, seeds. The rows are non-negotiable; this is the IG bit.

Thermomix

Everything except toppings into the bowl: 1 min / speed 8, scrape down, 30 sec / speed 6. Done. This recipe is the reason the machine lives on the counter.

Teen plate

Bigger scoop, double granola, and a slice of peanut-butter toast alongside on school-sport days.

Keeps

Blend and eat — it melts. Freeze banana chunks and portion berry bags ahead so mornings are 3 minutes.

Breakfast 02 · Weekend favourite

Banana Protein Pancakes with Blueberries & Honey

Blender pancakes with hidden cottage cheese — it vanishes completely and leaves only protein and a tender crumb. Nobody has ever detected it. Nobody ever will.

  • ⏱️ Prep: 8 min
  • 🔥 Cook: 15 min
  • 🍽️ Serves: 2 (10 pancakes)
435kcal
32gprotein
48gcarbs
12gfat

Ingredients

  • 100g porridge oats
  • 200g cottage cheese
  • 3 large eggs
  • 1 ripe banana (~120g)
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • ½ tsp cinnamon · pinch of salt
  • 10ml oil or butter, for the pan

To serve

  • 150g blueberries
  • 150g Greek yogurt · 20g honey

Method

  1. Blitz the oats to flour, then blend in the cottage cheese, eggs, banana, baking powder, vanilla, cinnamon and salt until smooth. Rest 5 minutes to thicken.
  2. Medium heat, nearly-dry non-stick pan. Drop heaped tablespoons; press 3–4 blueberries into each wet top.
  3. Flip when bubbles ring the edges (2–3 min); 90 seconds more. Keep the stack warm in a 90°C oven.
  4. Serve stacked with yogurt, remaining blueberries and honey ribboned at the table. Him: 4 pancakes. Her: the rest.

Thermomix

Oats: 10 sec / speed 9. Add everything else: 20 sec / speed 6. Rest the batter 5 min in the bowl, then pour straight from it.

Teen plate

Six pancakes, honey un-rationed, glass of whole milk. Double the batch and freeze her a stash — they toast from frozen.

Keeps

Fridge 3 days · freezer 2 months (flat, then bagged). Toaster beats microwave for reviving them.

Breakfast 03 · Zero-effort mornings

Overnight Oats, Three Ways — Apple Pie, Berry Ripple, Peanut Butter & Banana

One base, three jars, made in the time it takes the kettle to boil. Day two is the sweet spot. Build two jars every Sunday and Wednesday night and mornings look after themselves.

  • ⏱️ Prep: 8 min (night before)
  • 🔥 Cook: none
  • 🍽️ Serves: 2
~440kcal
30gprotein
52gcarbs
13gfat

The base (per 2 jars)

  • 90g porridge oats
  • 250g 0% fat Greek yogurt
  • 200ml milk
  • 10g honey · pinch of salt

Pick a way (per 2 jars)

  • Apple pie: 1 grated apple + 1 tsp cinnamon + 20g sultanas + 20g chopped walnuts on top
  • Berry ripple: 150g frozen berries microwaved 90 sec with 1 tsp honey, swirled through + 15g flaked almonds
  • PB & banana: 30g peanut butter stirred in + 1 sliced banana + cocoa dusting on top

Method

  1. Stir the base together — it should look sloppy; the oats drink it overnight.
  2. Fold in your chosen way’s stir-ins; divide between two jars; lid on; fridge.
  3. Morning: stir, loosen with a splash of milk if needed, add the fresh toppings. Eat from the jar — that’s the format.

Thermomix

Grate the apple for the apple-pie jars: 3 sec / speed 4. Otherwise this one’s a spoon job — the machine sits it out.

Teen plate

Her jar gets 60g oats to your 45g, whole milk, and the PB & banana version is basically engineered for her.

Keeps

Fridge 3 days. Don’t freeze. Nuts and banana go on in the morning, never overnight.

Breakfast 04 · Out-the-door food

Breakfast Burrito with Soft Scrambled Eggs, Charred Peppers & Avocado

A warm tortilla rolled tight around soft eggs, smoky peppers, cool avocado and a little melted cheese — structurally sound enough to eat one-handed on the school run.

  • ⏱️ Prep: 6 min
  • 🔥 Cook: 9 min
  • 🍽️ Serves: 2
450kcal
30gprotein
40gcarbs
19gfat

Ingredients

  • 5 large eggs
  • 1 red pepper, thin strips
  • 1 spring onion, sliced
  • 10ml olive oil
  • ½ avocado, sliced
  • 30g grated cheddar
  • 2 large wholemeal tortillas
  • 60g baby spinach
  • Pinch chilli flakes · squeeze of lemon · salt & pepper

Method

  1. Hot pan, half the oil: char the pepper strips 2 minutes untouched, toss with the spring onion 30 seconds, tip out.
  2. Low heat, rest of the oil: scramble the eggs slowly with a pinch of salt — stop while they still shine.
  3. Warm the tortillas 20 seconds a side in the dry pan (cold wraps crack; warm ones roll).
  4. Build down the centre: spinach first (moisture barrier), then eggs, cheese (so it melts against them), peppers, avocado, chilli, lemon.
  5. Fold bottom up, sides in, roll tight. Halve on the diagonal, or foil-wrap seam-down to go.

Thermomix

Not this one — eggs need a pan and a slow hand. (Grate the cheese in 3 sec / speed 7 if the block’s out.)

Teen plate

The other half of the avocado, extra cheese, and a banana in the bag. Match days: make hers a double — two smaller wraps roll better than one overstuffed one.

Keeps

Char the peppers the night before (fridge, 3 days). Assembled wraps go soft — build fresh, it’s 5 minutes.

Breakfast 05 · 10 minutes

Greek Yogurt Crunch Bowl with Warm Berry Compote

Cold thick yogurt, warm jammy berries, loud golden clusters. The warm-against-cold contrast is the whole trick, and it takes four minutes on a school morning.

  • ⏱️ Prep: 5 min
  • 🔥 Cook: 5 min
  • 🍽️ Serves: 2
420kcal
34gprotein
42gcarbs
13gfat

Ingredients

  • 300g 0% Greek yogurt (him) + 250g full-fat (her)
  • 200g frozen mixed berries
  • 10g honey + ½ lemon, juiced (compote)
  • 90g granola or oat-almond crunch
  • 80g fresh berries

Method

  1. Simmer the frozen berries, honey and lemon 4–5 minutes, crushing a few against the pan, until glossy and jammy.
  2. Thick cold yogurt into wide bowls; warm compote over so it bleeds at the edges.
  3. Fresh berries, then the crunch at the very last second so it stays loud. Never stir it in.

Thermomix

Triple-batch the compote: berries, honey, lemon — 8 min / 100°C / reverse / speed 1. Jar it; 30 seconds in the microwave brings it back warm all week.

Teen plate

Full-fat yogurt, half-again the granola, sliced banana under the compote.

Keeps

Compote: fridge 4 days, freezer 3 months (ice-cube trays). Bowls assemble in 90 seconds — no need to store.
