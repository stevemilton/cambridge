Lunch Bowls & Wraps

Bowl food: a base, a protein, colour, crunch and a drizzle. Most make brilliant next-day lunchboxes — cook once, eat twice. Macros are the adult portion.

Lunch 01 · The original bowl

Chicken Souvlaki Bowl with Lemon Rice & Tzatziki

Oregano-lemon chicken charred hard, lemony rice, cold tzatziki, crunchy salad. The bowl that started it all — make double, tomorrow’s lunch is done.

  • ⏱️ Prep: 15 min (+30 min marinating)
  • 🔥 Cook: 25 min
  • 🍽️ Serves: 2
545kcal
44gprotein
52gcarbs
16gfat

Ingredients

  • 350g chicken breast, 3cm chunks
  • 15ml olive oil + 1 lemon (zest & juice) + 2 garlic cloves + 2 tsp oregano + ½ tsp smoked paprika (marinade)
  • 130g basmati rice + 1 chicken stock cube
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley & dill
  • ½ cucumber + 150g cherry tomatoes + ¼ red onion + 6 olives + 1 tsp red wine vinegar (salad)

Tzatziki

  • 200g Greek yogurt
  • 100g cucumber, grated & squeezed dry
  • 1 garlic clove + 1 tsp red wine vinegar + 5ml olive oil + 1 tbsp mint

Method

  1. Toss the chicken in the marinade (30 min minimum, overnight is better).
  2. Tzatziki: squeeze the grated cucumber hard in your fist — this is the difference between tzatziki and yogurt soup — then stir everything together. Fridge.
  3. Cook the rice with the stock cube; fork through half the lemon juice and the herbs.
  4. Screaming-hot pan or griddle: chicken in one layer, 3 minutes untouched for char, turn, 3–4 more until cooked through.
  5. Build: rice, chicken one side, salad the other, tzatziki in the middle. Lemon and pepper over the top.

Thermomix

Tzatziki: garlic + mint 3 sec / speed 7, add squeezed cucumber, yogurt, vinegar, oil, 10 sec / reverse / speed 3. Rice: 900g water in the bowl, rinsed rice in the simmering basket, 16 min / 100°C / speed 4.

Teen plate

200–225g cooked rice to your 150g, plus a warm pitta. Same chicken, same tzatziki.

Keeps

Chicken 3 days · tzatziki 4 days · rice 24 hours (cool it fast). Raw marinated chicken freezes 3 months.

Lunch 02 · The IG one

Salmon Poke-Style Bowl with Sesame, Edamame & Sriracha Yogurt

Poke-shop energy, UK-supermarket reality: flash-seared soy-honey salmon cubes instead of raw fish, over rice with edamame, cucumber, avocado and a spicy drizzle. Section everything — the sections are the point.

  • ⏱️ Prep: 15 min
  • 🔥 Cook: 20 min (incl. rice)
  • 🍽️ Serves: 2
540kcal
38gprotein
55gcarbs
18gfat

Ingredients

  • 260g salmon fillet, skinned, 2.5cm cubes
  • 15ml soy sauce + 1 tsp honey + 1 tsp grated ginger + 1 garlic clove, crushed (glaze)
  • 130g basmati or sushi rice + 1 tsp rice/white wine vinegar
  • 100g frozen edamame, blanched
  • ½ cucumber, ribbons · 1 carrot, ribbons
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 1 tsp sesame seeds, toasted · spring onion, sliced

Sriracha yogurt

  • 100g Greek yogurt + 1–2 tsp sriracha + squeeze of lime

Method

  1. Cook the rice; fork the vinegar through while warm.
  2. Toss the salmon cubes in the glaze; sit 10 minutes while you ribbon the veg.
  3. Very hot non-stick pan: salmon cubes 60–90 seconds per side — 3 minutes total, sticky outside, just cooked inside. Don’t crowd, don’t overcook.
  4. Whisk the sriracha yogurt loose with a splash of water.
  5. Rice down, then sections: salmon, edamame, cucumber, carrot, avocado. Drizzle zig-zagged over, sesame and spring onion last.

Thermomix

Rice in the basket: 16 min / 100°C / speed 4 — drop the edamame into the water for the last 2 minutes. Drizzle: 5 sec / speed 3.

Teen plate

100g more cooked rice, bigger half of the avocado, sriracha to her tolerance. Prawn crackers on top and this becomes a requested meal.

Keeps

Rice 24h, salmon 2 days (good cold), avocado on the day. Buy salmon frozen — cheaper, identical here.

Lunch 03 · Fakeaway

Chicken Burrito Bowl with Black Beans, Charred Corn & Lime Crema

Everything the burrito chain sells, out of one pan and a chopping board: smoky lime chicken, black beans, blistered corn, fresh salsa and a lime-yogurt crema that does guac’s job at a tenth of the calories.

  • ⏱️ Prep: 15 min
  • 🔥 Cook: 20 min
  • 🍽️ Serves: 2
560kcal
44gprotein
58gcarbs
15gfat

Ingredients

  • 350g chicken breast, sliced
  • 1 tsp each smoked paprika & cumin + ½ tsp oregano + 1 lime, juiced + 10ml olive oil (rub)
  • 130g basmati rice
  • 1 × 400g tin black beans, drained, warmed with a pinch of cumin
  • 150g sweetcorn, charred in a dry pan
  • ½ avocado, sliced · Little Gem, shredded

Salsa & crema

  • 200g tomatoes + ¼ red onion + handful coriander + juice of ½ lime, chopped fine (salsa)
  • 100g Greek yogurt + zest & juice of ½ lime + pinch of salt (crema)

Method

  1. Rub the chicken; rice on to cook.
  2. Dry hot pan: char the corn 3–4 minutes until it pops and blackens in spots. Tip out.
  3. Same pan, the chicken: 6–8 minutes over high heat until burnished and cooked through.
  4. Chop the salsa; stir the crema.
  5. Rice, then sections: chicken, beans, corn, salsa, lettuce, avocado. Crema over the lot, lime wedge on the rim.

Thermomix

Salsa: tomatoes, onion, coriander, lime 3 sec / speed 4 — rubble, not soup. Crema: 5 sec / speed 3. Rice in the basket: 16 min / 100°C / speed 4.

Teen plate

More rice, the other avocado half, grated cheddar over the hot chicken, tortilla chips for scooping. Hers is basically the restaurant version.

Keeps

Everything but avocado and lettuce boxes well for next day — the definitive packed lunch of this page.

Lunch 04 · Street food Saturday

Chicken Gyros Pittas with Tzatziki & Pickled Slaw

Gyros energy without the rotating spit: paprika-oregano chicken strips, warm pittas, garlicky tzatziki and a sharp pink slaw. Assembly happens at the table — that’s half the fun.

  • ⏱️ Prep: 12 min
  • 🔥 Cook: 12 min
  • 🍽️ Serves: 2
520kcal
42gprotein
48gcarbs
15gfat

Ingredients

  • 320g chicken breast, finger-width strips
  • 10ml olive oil + 2 tsp oregano + 1 tsp smoked paprika + 2 garlic cloves + juice of ½ lemon (marinade)
  • 2 large wholemeal pittas
  • 150g tzatziki (see Souvlaki Bowl)
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced

Pickled slaw

  • 150g red cabbage, shredded + 1 carrot, grated + ¼ red onion
  • 1 tbsp red wine vinegar + ½ tsp honey + pinch of salt

Method

  1. Marinate the chicken strips (even 10 minutes helps).
  2. Scrunch the slaw ingredients together with your hands for a full minute until glossy and relaxed.
  3. Very hot dry pan: chicken in a single layer, 2–3 minutes per side, in two batches. Warm the pittas in the same pan after.
  4. To the table: pitta pockets, chicken, slaw, tomato, tzatziki. Build order: tzatziki on the walls, then chicken, then slaw. Napkins.

Thermomix

Slaw in seconds: cabbage chunks 4 sec / speed 4, carrot 3 sec / speed 4, then hand-scrunch with the vinegar. Tzatziki as per the Souvlaki Bowl.

Teen plate

Two pittas to your one. No other adaptation needed — it’s already teenager-perfect.

Keeps

Slaw improves for 2 days; cooked chicken 3 days; assembled pittas eat now.

Lunch 05 · 20 minutes with batch rice

Zesty Prawn Rice Bowl with Chilli, Lime & Avocado

Sweet garlicky prawns still warm from the pan over rice, with cold avocado, ribboned veg and a dressing that bites back. Somewhere between a poke bowl and the best prawn cocktail ever.

  • ⏱️ Prep: 12 min
  • 🔥 Cook: 8 min (+ rice)
  • 🍽️ Serves: 2
520kcal
36gprotein
55gcarbs
16gfat

Ingredients

  • 300g raw king prawns, patted very dry
  • 140g brown basmati rice
  • 10ml olive oil + 2 garlic cloves, sliced + ½ red chilli
  • 1 avocado · ½ cucumber & 1 carrot, ribboned
  • 100g edamame or peas, blanched
  • 15g toasted seeds · coriander

Dressing

  • 2 limes, juiced + 10ml soy + 1 tsp honey + 5ml olive oil + 1 tsp grated ginger

Method

  1. Rice on (brown takes ~25 min — or use yesterday’s batch, reheated piping hot).
  2. Shake the dressing — it should be sharp enough to make you blink; the rice softens it.
  3. Blazing pan, oil, prawns in one layer: 90 seconds untouched, then garlic and chilli in, 60–90 seconds more until the prawns curl into loose Cs. Loose, not tight.
  4. Dress the warm rice with a third of the dressing; arrange everything in sections; prawns and pan juices in the middle; rest of the dressing, seeds and coriander over.

Thermomix

Brown rice in the basket: 28–30 min / 100°C / speed 4. Prawns want a hot pan — don’t Thermomix a prawn.

Teen plate

100g more rice, most of the avocado, chilli on your half only if she prefers.

Keeps

Prawns 2 days, good cold. Frozen raw prawns are the buy — defrost in cold water in 15 minutes.

Lunch 06 · 20 minutes

Chicken Caesar Wraps with Yogurt Caesar & Griddled Little Gem

The Caesar, rebuilt: charred chicken, a yogurt dressing with real parmesan-anchovy backbone, and Little Gem kissed by the pan. Rolled, halved, gone.

  • ⏱️ Prep: 10 min
  • 🔥 Cook: 10 min
  • 🍽️ Serves: 2
495kcal
42gprotein
38gcarbs
17gfat

Ingredients

  • 320g chicken breast, butterflied
  • 5ml olive oil + 1 tsp oregano · salt & pepper
  • 2 large wholemeal tortillas
  • 2 Little Gem lettuces, halved lengthways
  • 150g cherry tomatoes, halved

Yogurt Caesar

  • 120g Greek yogurt
  • 15g parmesan, finely grated
  • 1 tsp Dijon + 1 small garlic clove + juice of ½ lemon
  • 2 anchovy fillets, mashed (optional, but it’s the backbone)

Method

  1. Whisk the Caesar; loosen with water to double-cream thickness.
  2. Hot griddle: oiled, seasoned chicken 3–4 minutes per side; rest, slice thick.
  3. Same pan: Little Gem halves cut-side down, 60 seconds — charred edges, crisp heart.
  4. Warm the wraps. Build: dressing, torn charred leaves, chicken, tomatoes, more dressing, parmesan dust. Roll tight, halve on the bias.

Thermomix

Dressing: parmesan chunk 10 sec / speed 10 first, then garlic, yogurt, Dijon, lemon, anchovy 10 sec / speed 6. Make double — it keeps 4 days.

Teen plate

A second wrap, or croutons in hers (cube yesterday’s bread, 8 min at 200°C with a little oil).

Keeps

Dressing 4 days · cooked chicken 3 days. Assembled wraps: eat now.

Lunch 07 · Veggie · The halloumi one

Halloumi & Roast Veg Couscous Bowl with Chickpeas & Honey-Lemon Drizzle

Golden squeaky halloumi over sweet roasted peppers, courgette and red onion with herby couscous and chickpeas — finished with a honey-lemon drizzle that makes the whole bowl glow.

  • ⏱️ Prep: 12 min
  • 🔥 Cook: 25 min
  • 🍽️ Serves: 2
510kcal
30gprotein
48gcarbs
22gfat

Ingredients

  • 160g halloumi, 1cm slices
  • 1 red pepper + 1 courgette + 1 red onion, chunks
  • 10ml olive oil + 1 tsp cumin
  • 100g wholewheat couscous + 150ml hot veg stock
  • 1 × 400g tin chickpeas, drained
  • 2 tbsp mint & parsley, chopped

Drizzle

  • 1 tsp honey + juice of ½ lemon + 5ml olive oil + pinch of chilli flakes

Method

  1. 210°C fan: veg tossed with oil, cumin and salt, roasted 22–25 minutes until the edges blacken sweetly. Chickpeas onto the tray for the last 10.
  2. Couscous: stock over, plate on top, 8 minutes, fork it light, fold in the herbs.
  3. Dry non-stick pan, medium-high: halloumi 90 seconds per side until deep gold. Cook it last — halloumi waits for no one.
  4. Couscous, roast veg and chickpeas, halloumi on top, drizzle over the hot cheese so it slicks everything.

Thermomix

No machine needed — but for batch prep, chop the roasting veg 4 sec / speed 4 in two loads.

Teen plate

Extra halloumi slices (they’re currency), 50% more couscous, warm flatbread.

Keeps

Roast veg + couscous 3 days — great cold. Halloumi: fry fresh, always.

Lunch 08 · Fakeaway

Chicken Satay Noodle Bowl with Crunchy Veg & Lime

Proper satay sauce — peanut butter, soy, lime, honey, a little chilli — clinging to chicken and noodles with a pile of crunchy vegetables. Tastes like the takeaway; reads like a training meal.

  • ⏱️ Prep: 15 min
  • 🔥 Cook: 15 min
  • 🍽️ Serves: 2
560kcal
44gprotein
52gcarbs
18gfat

Ingredients

  • 350g chicken breast, strips
  • 10ml olive oil
  • 120g wholewheat noodles (dry)
  • 1 red pepper, strips · 1 carrot, ribbons · 100g sugar snaps or green beans
  • 1 lime, wedges · coriander · 10g crushed peanuts

Satay sauce

  • 40g peanut butter (100% nuts)
  • 15ml soy sauce + 1 tsp honey
  • 1 garlic clove + 1 tsp grated ginger + juice of 1 lime
  • ¼ tsp chilli flakes + 80ml hot water

Method

  1. Whisk the satay sauce smooth — it thickens as it stands; keep it pourable.
  2. Noodles per packet; drain, cold rinse, toss with a few drops of oil.
  3. Hot wok: chicken in the oil, 5–6 minutes until golden and cooked. Veg in for 2 minutes — bright and still snappy.
  4. Noodles in, sauce over, one minute of proper tossing until everything is coated and glossy.
  5. Bowls: crushed peanuts, coriander, lime wedge. Squeeze before eating.

Thermomix

Satay sauce: garlic + ginger 3 sec / speed 7, scrape, add everything else — 2 min / 50°C / speed 3. Silky every time; make double, fridge a jar.

Teen plate

160g noodles instead of 120g and give her the difference; extra peanuts; chilli to taste.

Keeps

Sauce 5 days in a jar (loosen with hot water). Assembled bowl: next-day lunchbox, eaten cold like sesame noodles.
