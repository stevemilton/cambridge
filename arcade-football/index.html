How it plays

Two inputs. One thumb. About ten seconds a goal.

1

Beat the shieldwall

You run at goal automatically. Drag your thumb left and right to slip past the defenders closing in. No buttons, no stick.

2

Swipe to strike

Reach the box and time drops into slow motion. The arc of your swipe sets the placement and the curve — the keeper reads the obvious ones.

3

Keep the streak

Every goal speeds the game up and puts another defender in your way. Your streak is your score. One mistake ends it.
